# Look

What the reference shots do, as vocabulary, then what Glowline does with it.
The refs set the tier. Glowline's setting is its own: a rooftop circuit at blue
hour, where the paint glows.

## What the refs show

- **ref-01 overview.** One soft key light from upper left, long soft shadows
  under every tree, block and prop, darker contact at every base. Nothing is a
  flat colour: the road has corrugation lines, the grass has mottled patches,
  the table has wood grain. The circuit sits inside a bigger place (a craft
  table with tools around it), so the frame is full edge to edge. Prop density
  is high and varied in scale: dozens of trees, signs, sheds, a bridge.
- **ref-02 grid.** Eight karts, each a multi-part model with its own colour,
  number flag and driver; each casts a shadow. A start gantry with lights. The
  trackside wall is hundreds of individual rounded blocks in six colours with
  bevels and soft shading. The far background (shelves, window, lamp) is real
  3D, softened by depth. Painted grid boxes and tyre marks on the road.
- **ref-03 race.** Chase camera low behind the kart. Road surface texture
  reads at speed; the edge has a patterned kerb strip and a truss rail.
  Mid-ground has benches, signposts, a tree, an item box row; background is
  the room. Speed lines at the screen edge.
- **ref-04 garage.** Kart fills the frame: thick edges, printed decals,
  layered parts, soft shadow on the floor. Stat bars per kart, a five-step
  header, a colour picker.
- **HUD grammar (02, 03).** Every panel is an object from the game's world
  with its own shape, texture and drop shadow: standings list down the left
  with colour chips and names, lap + time panel top right, analogue
  speedometer and a round position badge bottom right, framed minimap bottom
  left, a counter and an item slot top left, gap-to-next readout. One display
  typeface with character. Nothing is a default rounded rectangle.
- **Post.** Soft ambient occlusion in creases and under objects, gentle bloom
  on brights, slight vignette, warm grade. Shadows are soft-edged.

## Glowline's translation

- **Setting.** A circuit laid across city rooftops and an elevated deck at
  blue hour: deep blue zenith, warm band at the horizon, the first windows lit.
  Bright enough that materials and shadows read, dark enough that paint glows.
- **Key light.** Low warm sun from one side, soft-edged shadows from every
  kart, barrier, prop and building. Cool sky fill. Lamp pools on the road.
- **Materials.** Asphalt with aggregate grain, patches, cracks and painted
  markings; concrete with stains and panel joints; painted metal barriers with
  chipped edges; rubber; glass. Roughness varies inside every surface.
- **Density.** The circuit sits in a district: buildings beside the track with
  rooftop clutter, a grandstand and pit wall at the line, barrier blocks,
  tyre stacks, cones, signs, lamps, banners, trees in planters.
- **Karts.** Multi-part, readable from behind at chase distance: body, nose,
  side pods, wing, roll hoop, driver, wheels with rims, number plate.
- **Paint.** The signature: wet, glossy, emissive strokes with a soft glow on
  nearby surfaces. Brightest thing on screen besides lamps.
- **HUD.** Trackside timing hardware: panels that look like lit signage and
  enamel plates, one condensed italic display face, tabular numerals.
- **Post.** Ambient occlusion, bloom keyed to paint and lamps, vignette, a
  teal-and-amber grade.
