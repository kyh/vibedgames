# Tabletop Turbo: art verdict R4

**Overall: FAIL (6 of 9 pass, up from 5).** The title now reads, the HUD lost its box and bar, the hoop is in the track ramp and coins glint. Still short of the references: rooms are bare floor with trinkets, paint stacks into a rainbow, and no effect reads at a glance in any race still.

## Scores

**C1 Pixel/splat fidelity: PASS (narrow).** Tabletop frames hold hard pixels, dabbed track tone and lumpy kerbs (r4-play, whole curve). r4-chase still breaks: the near kerbs are 150-400 px cream/brown blotches, and the back wall is a per-pixel vertical stipple (top third, x 130-1280), the "dotted grid" style.md warns against.

**C2 Palette discipline: FAIL.** r4-kitchen top-left carries seven parallel stripes (sky, white, charcoal, teal, yellow, lavender, pink); r4-playroom top-left has six. Add the lime boost pad, a blue plate and blue-banded mugs and the kitchen frame has no dominant accent. r4-play, with three trails, is the only frame at reference discipline.

**C3 Composition/readability: PASS (narrow).** The player is found in the three tabletop frames. Faults: r4-kitchen's six-kart clump (centre, 500-690 x 230-490) is one dark mass; the minimap is drawn on top of the toast plate (bottom-left); r4-playroom's blocks and dominoes sit under "8TH" (bottom-right); in r4-chase the diamond floats ~100 px above the teal kart and reads as marking the grey kart behind it, while a 210 px purple kart fills the foreground.

**C4 World richness/charm: FAIL.** r4-play is planks, two cones, a duck cut by the left edge and blocks cut by the right and bottom edges. r4-playroom is about half empty navy tile with one sponge. Kitchen is best (mugs, toast, sugar) but still scattered objects on floor. Nothing room-scale frames a shot; sheep-dog's fence and air-hockey's spectators stage every edge.

**C5 Kart craft: PASS.** Three distinct chassis read in r4-garage (winged coupe, block-nosed hot rod, open-wheeler), and the line of seven in r4-playroom keeps eight hues separable. Overlapping karts still merge without a rim (r4-kitchen clump; pink over teal in r4-chase).

**C6 VFX/juice: FAIL (narrow).** Coin glints are there (r4-play bottom-left, one white pixel pair per coin) and teal spray surrounds the player (r4-play, 700-770 x 150-230). But dust is orange specks on orange track (r4-playroom, 310-480 x 180-290; round orange dots in r4-chase at 590-725 x 460-700), not cream, and there are no streaks or paint glints I can point to. Nothing matches a reference's "+100" or spark burst for legibility.

**C7 HUD: PASS.** Timer box and "YOU" bar are gone; numerals are chunky and in palette. Remaining: "?" is a thin non-pixel glyph (top-left, all frames); "/10", "LAP", "/3", "SLICK" are below the art grid; the boost meter is a near-invisible grey dash (x 1155-1265, y 603).

**C8 Lighting/depth: PASS.** Stepped kerb walls, chunky shadows under cones, mugs and the hoop. Kart shadows remain thin slivers on orange; r4-chase floor has no shadow structure.

**C9 Title screen: PASS (narrow).** r4-garage: chunky logo, three karts in card order, picked kart teal and larger, controls in a legible panel. Faults: a red prop is cut by the top edge (x 460-560); the flag pole runs behind and out of the controls panel; silhouettes keep grey and white parts; no podium.

## Punch list (by impact)

1. **Room-scale hero piece per circuit touching two frame edges** (bed leg plus book stack, cereal box, toy chest), with small props grouped at its foot. (C4)
2. **Cap visible paint at three stripes per track width;** newest overwrites, older fades to orange within a lap. Recolour the boost pad to cream chevrons. (C2)
3. **Make dust cream and bigger:** 3-4 art-pixel puffs two ramp steps lighter than the track, plus two cream streaks behind any fast kart; add a white 2-pixel glint row in fresh paint. (C6)
4. **Self-coloured dark rim and a wide dark-brown blob shadow per kart,** so piles separate. (C3, C5, C8)
5. **Prop exclusion zones** under all four HUD corners and at frame edges; move the kitchen plate off the minimap and the playroom blocks off the position readout. (C3, C4)
6. **Chase camera:** raise and pull back so no kart exceeds 160 px, anchor the diamond directly over the player's roof, replace the wall stipple with flat bands plus a full-width skirting board. (C1, C3)
7. **Quieter bedroom floor:** drop the plank shade blotches to one ramp step of contrast so the track owns the frame. (C2, C4)
8. **Title and HUD polish:** podium strip under the karts, pure brown silhouettes, remove the cropped red prop, shift the flag clear of the controls; pixel-font "?" and small labels; give the empty boost meter a dark outline. (C9, C7)

## Reachability

C2 and C6 are colour, fade and particle work: procedural primitives reach a pass. C4 can reach a narrow pass with box/cylinder furniture; reference-level charm (plush toys, spectators with faces, a slipper) needs authored or generated models.
