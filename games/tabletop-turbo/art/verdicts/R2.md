# Tabletop Turbo: art verdict R2

**Overall: FAIL (4 of 9 pass, up from 2).** The player marker, cast shadows, bigger karts and the pixel-font HUD landed. What still separates this from the references: no room has a palette, no room is a staged place, the HUD carries twelve elements where the references carry three, and the title is still a race frame under panels.

## Scores

**C1 Pixel/splat fidelity: PASS (narrow).** Tabletop frames hold hard pixels, blobby track tone and lumpy kerbs (r2-play, whole curve). It breaks when the camera gets close: r2-chase's foreground kart and road are smeared camouflage blotches, and r2-garage's hero kart is mush. r2-playroom has stray blue specks along the outer kerb (upper right).

**C2 Palette discipline: FAIL.** r2-kitchen: orange track, cream/brown checker, grey tile with dark grout, yellow box, blue-banded mugs, purple gift boxes, yellow coins, plus seven paint stripes side by side (left third). Kerbs alone use three schemes in r2-play (cream/brown, red/orange, pink/maroon lower right). Kart hues are not eight distinct: navy, blue and purple sit adjacent in r2-kitchen (centre clump) and r2-playroom, where navy also vanishes into the blue floor.

**C3 Composition/readability: PASS (narrow).** The cyan diamond finds the teal kart at once in r2-play, r2-kitchen and r2-playroom, and the road reads. Faults remain: r2-chase has no marker and a purple kart fills the foreground in front of the camera; r2-kitchen's grout lattice is louder than the track; props sit under HUD (mug under the minimap, a cropped prop under "8TH", r2-play's yellow blob under the leaderboard).

**C4 World richness/charm: FAIL.** Mugs, sugar cubes, a crayon and blocks are a real gain, but they are small items scattered on bare floor. Nothing is at room scale and nothing frames the shot; r2-playroom shows floor, track and two cropped slivers. r2-chase's top 40% is flat tan with a row of floating dots. Peg-drop, air-hockey and sheep-dog fill every edge with bespoke staging.

**C5 Kart craft: PASS (narrow).** At about 100 px (r2-play centre) wheels, stripe, spoiler and two body types (boxy white, open-wheel teal) read. Overlapping karts merge with no rim between them (white on teal in r2-play; four-kart clump at left in r2-playroom).

**C6 VFX/juice: FAIL.** Four race frames at 150+ km/h show paint, a marker and grey smoke lumps that read as rocks (r2-play, top centre). No boost flame, speed streaks, drift glow or coin sparkle, which are persistent effects a still would catch.

**C7 HUD: FAIL (narrow).** Large numerals now match the reference font. But twelve elements remain (?, E, item box, coins, three-row board, SOLO, lap, timer, track name, minimap, gap, speed, position, SLICK), the micro labels ("/10", "KM/H", "SOLO", "E") are drawn finer than the art grid and are near-illegible, and the timer still sits in a translucent box.

**C8 Lighting/depth: PASS.** Chunky cast shadows under the block (r2-play right), mugs and box (r2-kitchen); kerb walls step in tone. Kart shadows are still faint on orange.

**C9 Title screen: FAIL.** r2-garage: one kart seen from behind as an unreadable teal blob on blotchy orange, plain text logo in a navy box, six navy panels including a controls wall, red/blue/green stat bars from no palette.

## Punch list (by impact)

1. **Lock one ramp set per room (16 colours) and quiet the floor.** Kitchen grout one step from the tile, not near-black; playroom carpet desaturated and darker. One kerb scheme everywhere (cream/brown checker); delete the red, pink and maroon stripes. Coins and item boxes take the room accent. **This moves the most criteria: C2, C3, C4, C7.**
2. **Respace the eight kart hues** by value as well as hue: teal, white, lime, pink, yellow, sky blue, violet, charcoal. Drop navy and one of blue/purple.
3. **Cap paint at two or three stripes across the track width;** newest overwrites, hard edges. Kills the kitchen rainbow.
4. **Title as a diorama:** selected kart three-quarter front, small enough to stay crisp (under 160 px), on a shelf or podium with the other two body types beside it; chunky logo built in scene pixels; controls moved to a help page.
5. **Cut the HUD to position, lap, time, item.** Remove SOLO, E, track name, gap, KM/H label and the timer box; nothing smaller than one art pixel.
6. **One room-scale hero piece per circuit touching the frame edge** (bed leg and slipper, cereal box and table edge, toy chest), with small props grouped beside it.
7. **Persistent juice:** boost flame at the exhaust, two or three speed streaks behind karts over 140 km/h, drift-charge colour under rear wheels, smoke tinted in the kart's paint colour.
8. **Self-coloured dark rim on every kart** and a darker, wider blob shadow, so overlapping karts separate.
9. **Chase camera:** raise and pull back so no kart exceeds about 180 px, cull karts between camera and player, show the marker, replace the flat sky with a wall or skirting band, remove the dot arc.
10. **Keep props out of HUD corners and off frame edges;** fix the blue specks on the playroom kerb.
