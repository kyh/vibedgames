# Tabletop Turbo: art verdict R3

**Overall: FAIL (5 of 9 pass, up from 4).** Kerbs are unified, the kitchen grout is quiet, the HUD is lighter and the title finally shows three karts. Still short of the references: the rooms are bare floor with scattered trinkets, there is no juice in any of four race stills, paint still stacks into a rainbow, and the title is a race frame with text spilled over it.

## Scores

**C1 Pixel/splat fidelity: PASS (narrow).** Tabletop frames are clean: hard pixels, blobby track tone, lumpy checker kerbs (r3-play, whole curve). r3-chase still breaks: the cropped blue kart bottom-right is ~250 px of smeared camouflage and the near road is blotches, not splats.

**C2 Palette discipline: FAIL.** One kerb scheme now, and the grout no longer shouts. But r3-kitchen's left third carries seven parallel stripes (white, sky, charcoal, teal, yellow, lavender, pink), plus a black/yellow hoop, a hazard ramp, a lime boost pad and a blue crayon. Most karts share a navy secondary (r3-kitchen centre clump: navy/yellow, navy/blue, navy/pink read as one dark mass). References hold one accent family.

**C3 Composition/readability: PASS.** The cyan diamond finds the player in all four race frames, including r3-chase now. Faults: r3-chase puts a ~180 px purple kart directly over the player; props still sit under HUD (red/yellow blob under the leaderboard in r3-play, sugar cubes under "?" and coins in r3-playroom) and are cropped by frame edges (mug in r3-kitchen bottom, blocks in r3-playroom right).

**C4 World richness/charm: FAIL.** r3-play is planks, one cone and a blob; r3-playroom is 60% empty navy tile. Nothing at room scale, nothing framing the shot. r3-chase shows a row of props on the horizon and an unreadable brown slab with red/white ends. Sheep-dog's fenced pen and air-hockey's spectators fill every edge with staging; this fills none.

**C5 Kart craft: PASS (narrow).** Two body types, stripes, wings and wheels read at ~100 px (r3-play centre, r3-garage). Overlaps still merge: white into teal in r3-play, the five-kart pile in r3-playroom centre has no rim separating bodies.

**C6 VFX/juice: FAIL.** Four race stills, zero effects beyond paint. No speed streaks, drift glow, coin sparkle or dust. The teal lumps above the player in r3-play read as barrels on the track, not smoke. References land "GOAL!", "+100" and spark bursts in ordinary frames.

**C7 HUD: PASS (narrow).** Chunky numerals in palette colours, nine elements, corners mostly clear. Still wrong: translucent timer box (top right, all race frames), "/10", "LAP" and "SLICK" drawn below the art grid, a thin non-pixel "?", and a heavy white bar behind "YOU".

**C8 Lighting/depth: PASS.** Stepped kerb walls, chunky shadows under the cone (r3-play) and hoop (r3-kitchen). Kart shadows remain faint on orange.

**C9 Title screen: FAIL (narrow).** r3-garage: chunky logo and three three-quarter karts are right. But the controls list is printed straight over the checkered flag pole and is unreadable (right, y 530-610); the highlighted card is DART (left) while the coloured kart is rightmost, so selection does not read; silhouettes are navy lumps on orange; cards are navy panels; a duck is cropped at the top edge. No podium, no staging.

## Punch list (by impact)

1. **Room-scale hero piece per circuit, touching two frame edges.** Bedroom: bed leg plus a book stack; kitchen: cereal box and table edge; playroom: toy chest. Box/cylinder builds, 3-4 m tall, one ramp each, with small props grouped at its foot and bare floor elsewhere. (C4, C3)
2. **Cap paint at three stripes across the width;** newest overwrites, older fades to track orange after a lap. Recolour hoop, hazard ramp and boost pad to cream/brown plus one room accent. (C2)
3. **Drop the shared navy secondary.** Each kart's dark parts use its own hue's bottom ramp step; add a one-step-darker self-coloured rim and a wider, darker blob shadow. (C2, C5, C8)
4. **Persistent juice:** two or three cream speed streaks behind any kart over 140 km/h, drift-charge colour step under rear wheels, a 4-pixel sparkle cycling on coins, smoke as small flat puffs in the kart's paint tint that shrink within 0.3 s. (C6)
5. **Title staging:** karts on a cream podium strip, selected kart centred and 1.2x, card order matching kart order, silhouettes in dark brown from the track ramp; move controls to the "?" page; remove the duck or seat it fully in frame. (C9)
6. **Chase camera:** raise and pull back so no kart exceeds 160 px, fade or cull karts within 2 m of the lens, replace the top band with a skirting board and wallpaper stripe. (C1, C3)
7. **HUD cleanup:** remove the timer box and "YOU" bar (tint the text cyan instead), redraw "/10", "LAP", "SLICK", "?" at one art pixel minimum. (C7)
8. **Prop exclusion zones:** no prop spawns under the four HUD corners or within 1 m of the visible frame edge unless it is the hero piece. (C3, C4)

## Reachability

Procedural primitives are enough for C2, C6, C7, C9 and the chase half of C1; those are colour, camera, layout and particle work. C4 can reach a narrow pass with box/cylinder furniture (bed leg, cereal box, chest, books). Reference-level charm (slipper, plush toy, spectators with faces) needs authored models. C5's remaining gap closes with rims and shadows; a third truly distinct silhouette would need authoring.
