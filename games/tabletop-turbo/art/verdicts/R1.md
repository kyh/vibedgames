# Tabletop Turbo: art verdict R1

**Overall: FAIL (2 of 9 pass).** The splat rendering works. The art direction does not: every reference commits to one small palette and one staged diorama; this is a rainbow toy bin with a dashboard HUD bolted on top.

## Scores

**C1 Pixel/splat fidelity: PASS (narrow).** Hard pixels, lumpy kart and prop silhouettes throughout. But the track is polygon-straight (r1-play: ruler-clean kerb edges and flat orange slab), and the HUD is drawn at a finer grid than the scene (speedometer ring, minimap line in every race capture), which breaks the single-grid look the references hold.

**C2 Palette discipline: FAIL.** No scene has a palette. r1-play: saturated orange track + red/white kerb + magenta box + royal-blue cube + yellow duck + kelly-green trees + teal/lime paint on brown wood. r1-kitchen-grid adds six paint stripes and confetti. References (air-hockey: purple/pink/teal/yellow; knight-dodge: 8 rust tones; mine-cart: 4) never exceed one accent family.

**C3 Composition/readability: FAIL.** r1-playroom: eight karts stacked in one diagonal clump, the player indistinguishable; nothing marks "you". r1-kitchen-grid: paint pots and beach balls in the foreground cover the track and the grid, the gantry pole bisects the karts. r1-chase: the player kart is purple with a teal dome, yet the HUD says you are teal. HUD panels eat all four corners.

**C4 World richness/charm: FAIL.** Props are primitives scattered at random: cones-as-trees, cubes, beach balls, a duck, repeated in all three rooms. "Bedroom", "playroom" and "kitchen" differ only by floor texture; there is no bed leg, no table edge, no cereal box, nothing at room scale. References stage a bespoke, themed frame (peg-drop's cabinet, sheep-dog's fenced pen and pond).

**C5 Kart craft: FAIL.** At tabletop size (r1-play, centre) karts are ~60 px blobs of two tones with black wheel lumps; body types cannot be told apart. r1-garage close-up is a pile of overlapping karts cropped by panels; no single clean silhouette is shown anywhere.

**C6 VFX/juice: FAIL.** Paint trails are the one good idea (r1-play, cyan streak). Otherwise: grey smoke circles, nothing else. No sparks, speed lines, coin pops, "+1" text, drift-charge glow, boost flame, countdown numerals or position-change burst. The start grid (r1-kitchen-grid) has no "3-2-1". Air-hockey's GOAL! burst and peg-drop's +100 have no equivalent.

**C7 HUD: FAIL.** Dark navy panels with a thin UI font, drop shadows and a round analogue speedometer belong to a different game. Navy (#1a1a2e-ish) and mint appear nowhere in the scenes. The 8-row leaderboard, item box, coin pill, lap panel, minimap, position card, gap pill and dial cover roughly a quarter of r1-play. References: chunky pixel font in palette colours, no panels, three or four numbers.

**C8 Lighting/depth: PASS (narrow).** Two to three shade steps on props and kerb walls, track side wall darker. But cast shadows are absent in r1-play/r1-playroom (karts and trees float), and even in "shadows on" r1-kitchen-grid they barely register. Sheep-dog and marble-tilt ground every object with a chunky shadow.

**C9 Title screen: FAIL.** r1-garage is a race frame behind four text boxes. Two instruction walls (rules panel and controls panel) cover the right half; the karts being selected are cropped and half hidden under the controls panel; the logo is plain text in a navy box.

## Punch list (by impact)

1. **One palette per room, 16 colours max.** Bedroom: warm wood, cream, track orange, one accent. Requantise props to that room's ramps; delete magenta, royal blue and kelly green from the bedroom.
2. **Demote the track orange.** Desaturate one step and reduce kerb contrast (cream/dull red) so paint and karts are the loudest things in frame.
3. **Kart colours = paint colours, eight distinct hues, single-hue ramps.** Fix the purple-kart/teal-HUD mismatch in chase view.
4. **Mark the player.** A bouncing arrow or "YOU" tag in pixel font above the kart, plus a light outline ring; scale all karts up ~1.4x in tabletop view.
5. **Rebuild the HUD on the art grid.** Chunky pixel font in palette colours, no navy panels, no drop shadows. Keep: position (large, bottom-left), lap, time, item. Drop the analogue dial for a number; shrink the leaderboard to three rows (ahead / you / behind) or hide it.
6. **Cast shadows always on,** chunky, one ramp step darker than the floor, under karts, props and track supports.
7. **Themed set dressing at room scale.** Bedroom: bed leg, slipper, book stack ramp, crayon barriers. Kitchen: cereal box, mug, spilled sugar. Playroom: toy chest, train track. Remove generic cones-as-trees and duplicate beach balls.
8. **Keep props out of the lens.** No prop between camera and track (kitchen foreground pots); cluster dressing in two or three composed groups, leave floor empty elsewhere.
9. **Juice kit:** pixel sparks on wall contact, "+1" coin pops, boost flame and speed streaks, drift-charge colour steps under the wheels, paint splatter at overtakes, big "3 2 1 GO!" and "LAP 2" banners in the scene font.
10. **Give the track surface splat texture:** blobby tone variation, lumpy kerb edge, visible plastic joins and clips, so it stops reading as a flat polygon.
11. **Title as a diorama.** Three karts on a shelf or podium, selected one centred, lit and rotating, logo built in chunky pixel letters with paint dripping; move rules/controls to a "how to play" page.
12. **Limit paint noise.** Cap at the two or three most recent layers per patch, hard edges, no six-stripe rainbow at the grid; drop the confetti.
